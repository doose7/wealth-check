Common questions

How do I restore my purchase?
Open the unlock screen and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.

How does syncing work?
With the unlock, your data syncs between your devices through your own iCloud account. Make sure iCloud is turned on for Wealth Check in the Settings app.

Where do exchange rates come from?
Rates are fetched online so each asset can be converted to your main currency. Only the currency codes are requested; none of your values are sent.

Can I import my existing data?
Yes. With the unlock you can import from Excel, CSV or a Google Sheets link, and export your data at any time.
